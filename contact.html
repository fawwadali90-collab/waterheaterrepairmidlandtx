There is no contact form to fill out and no ticket queue to wait in. Just a direct request that puts you in touch with a professional who works on water heaters in the Permian Basin every day.

What to Include When You Request Service

The more clearly you describe the problem, the faster we can match you with the right contractor and the better prepared they will be when they arrive. You do not need to diagnose anything yourself. Just tell us what you see, hear, and notice.

Start with the basics. Is it a tank or tankless unit, and is it gas or electric? Then describe the symptom in plain words. No hot water at all, water that runs out too fast, a puddle or damp spot under the tank, popping or rumbling noises, rusty colored hot water, or an error code flashing on a tankless display are all useful details.

  • Your name and the best way for the contractor to reach you
  • Your address or neighborhood in the Midland area
  • Tank or tankless, and gas or electric if you know
  • What the unit is doing wrong, in your own words
  • How urgent it is, such as an active leak versus a maintenance visit

What Happens After You Request Service

Once we receive your request, we connect you with an independent contractor in our network who serves your location. The contractor will reach out to you directly to confirm the details and schedule a visit at a time that works for you.

At the visit, the contractor inspects the unit, explains what they find, and gives you a quote for the repair or replacement before any work begins. You decide whether to move forward. There is no obligation to accept the quote, and you are always free to ask questions until the options make sense.

For urgent problems like a leaking tank or a complete loss of hot water, let us know it is an emergency when you request service so we can prioritize matching you with a contractor who offers emergency response.
